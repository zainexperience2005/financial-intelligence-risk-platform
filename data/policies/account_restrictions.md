# Account Restriction Policy

Policy ID: FIN-POL-003
Version: 1.0

## Restriction Requirements

Account restrictions must not be applied solely because a
transaction exceeds a monetary threshold.

Investigators should consider transaction evidence, applicable
policy, and available customer history.

## Approval Requirement

Freezing or restricting a customer account requires explicit
human approval.

An AI agent may recommend an account restriction but must not
execute the restriction without approval.

## Audit Requirement

The investigation evidence, recommendation, approval decision,
and resulting action must be recorded in the audit trail.
