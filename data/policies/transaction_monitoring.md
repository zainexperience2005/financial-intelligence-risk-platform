# Transaction Monitoring Policy

Policy ID: FIN-POL-001
Version: 1.0

## High-Value Transfers

A transfer with an amount of PKR 400,000 or more must be
flagged for additional review.

The review should consider transaction amount, destination
country, customer activity, and recent transaction patterns.

A high-value transfer does not automatically indicate fraud.

## International Transfers

International transfers with an amount of PKR 300,000 or more
require enhanced transaction review.

Analysts should consider whether the transfer is consistent
with the customer's available transaction history.

## Repeated High-Value Activity

Multiple high-value transfers within a short period should be
reviewed together rather than independently.

Repeated activity may increase the investigation priority.
