# Failed Transaction Investigation Policy

Policy ID: FIN-POL-002
Version: 1.0

## Failure Investigation

A material increase in failed transactions should be
investigated using transaction status, failure reason,
transaction type, amount, and time period.

## Processor Failures

Transactions failing because of processor or infrastructure
timeouts should be separated from customer-caused failures.

## Risk Review Failures

Transactions with a failure reason of risk_review require
additional investigation before any customer-impacting action
is recommended.

A failed transaction alone is not sufficient evidence of
fraud.
